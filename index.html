What BarberBook Pro covers

📅 Booking & chair flow

Reduce double-books and gaps. See who’s in the chair, who’s next, and walk-in overflow at a glance.

👤 Client cards

Cut history, notes, photos (with consent), and preferred styles so every visit feels personal.

💈 Services & pricing

Fade tiers, beard add-ons, memberships — version your menu and keep staff aligned.

📦 Retail & tips

Track product moves and payouts. Optional tip pools for teams that share the floor.

Shop workflow

  1. Open — Staff clock in, chairs go live.
  2. Book — Online + walk-in in one queue.
  3. Cut — Timer-aware handoffs between barbers.
  4. Close — Daily recap: revenue, rebooks, retail.
