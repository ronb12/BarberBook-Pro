Features

Everything the front desk and the floor need.

The experience stays focused on real barbershop work: keep the queue moving, keep client details organized, and make checkout cleaner at the end of each service.

Appointments and walk-ins

Handle booked cuts and same-day traffic side by side, with quick entry for guests, service selection, and barber assignment.

Clients and barber team

Keep client preferences, barber specialties, and team structure visible so the shop can move quickly without losing context.

Checkout and payment handoff

Generate payment-ready totals, open configured payment links, and copy booking or payment details when a client needs them fast.

Workflow

A simple flow for a busy shop.

Step 1

Book or add to queue

Start with an appointment or a walk-in, choose the service, and put the client with the right barber.

Step 2

Run the day from the dashboard

See who is scheduled, how the queue looks, which services are moving, and how revenue is trending.

Step 3

Finish checkout cleanly

Use the payment handoff tools to complete the service and share payment or booking info without slowing the chair down.

Launch

Ready to step into the shop dashboard?

Owners can sign in, add shop settings, wire payment details, and start using the full management app from the same install.
